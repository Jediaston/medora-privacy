Medora Privacy Policy

Publisher: Arkë Pi · App: Medora (com.arkepi.medora)
Last updated: 2026-09-19

Medora is an on-device directory of DOH RLED–licensed health facilities across Western Visayas (Region 6). It is not medical advice.

Data we collect

Medora does not create user accounts and does not collect personal health information (PHI), names, emails, phone numbers, or precise location for our servers.

Facility data shown in the app comes from a bundled catalog derived from the DOH Western Visayas RLED licensed health facility list. Search and filters run on your device.

Network use

When online, the app may fetch an updated copy of the facility catalog. Fonts (Inter, Poppins) and icon fonts (Font Awesome) ship inside the app package; the WebView does not load those from Google Fonts or cdnjs CDNs. Those requests do not include your medical records or an Arkë Pi account profile — there is none.

Permissions

Medora does not require account, contacts, camera, microphone, or continuous location permissions for its core directory function. System intents (for example opening a link) follow your device settings.

Children

Medora is a general-audience reference directory. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children’s personal data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the publisher Arkë Pi via the developer contact details listed on the Google Play store listing for Medora.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
